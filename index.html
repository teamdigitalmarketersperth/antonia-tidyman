A Life Beautifully Lived

Born in the village of Siġġiewi, Malta, into a family of thirteen children, Antonia’s life was a remarkable journey of courage, resilience and love. She arrived in Australia at just nine years old, with little more than hope for a better future, and grew into a fiercely determined woman who never shied away from life’s challenges.

She worked tirelessly to give her children, Lisa and Stephen, the childhood she had always wanted for them: family gatherings, holidays, laughter and opportunities. She treasured her family, her lifelong friendships and the special bond she shared with her twin sister. Becoming a grandmother to Sofia and Isaac brought her immeasurable happiness.

With her wonderful sense of humour, generous heart and unmistakable strength, Antonia had a way of making those around her feel loved and cared for. Her greatest legacy is the love she gave, the lives she enriched, and the family who will carry her spirit forward.

No matter how difficult life became, she always found a way to make things happen.

A Story She Left Behind

Secrets of a Maltese Girl

A story of poverty, resilience and the untold secrets of a large immigrant family

Some stories are too meaningful to be forgotten.

In her memoir, Antonia told her own story: the crowded household of 1960s Malta, the journey to Australia at nine years old, and a life built on fierce determination and a belief in something better. It is a lasting part of her legacy, and a way for family, friends and generations to come to hear her voice.

“It is my hope that my children and their children will one day read this book and gain a profound understanding of the roots from which they came.”

Antonia, on why she wrote it

A Place to Remember

Wherever life takes us, her memories remain close.

Antonia’s final resting place is Castlebrook Memorial Park, Rouse Hill NSW. The location within the park is to be confirmed.

The family intends for a small QR code at her resting place to lead back to this page, so that whenever you visit, in person or from far away, her story, her photographs and your memories are here to return to.

A heartfelt thank you

Our family would like to thank everyone who filled Mum’s life with laughter, love and friendship, and who brought her comfort and joy in her final days.

To the wonderful nurses and carers who looked after Mum with such compassion, kindness and dignity, we are deeply grateful.

To everyone who visited, brought meals, shared memories, held her hand, sat quietly beside her or simply held space with her, thank you. Every act of kindness, no matter how small, reminded us just how special Mum was and how deeply she was loved.

With love, always.

Antonia Tidyman
